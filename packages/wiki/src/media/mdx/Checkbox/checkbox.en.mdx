The `Checkbox` component is an interactive form control for binary, value-variant, or indeterminate state selection with integrated validation, descriptions, and accessibility support.

It wraps a native `<input type="checkbox">` inside a semantic `<label>`, coordinating two-way `v-model` and `v-model:selected` synchronization, read-only click protection (`aria-readonly`), custom checked/indeterminate icons, loading indicators, and skeleton placeholders.

**Key features:**

- **Three States:** Checked, unchecked, and indeterminate visual states
- **Model & Variant Sync:** Two-way `v-model` boolean binding and `v-model:selected` with `valueVariant` / `valueVariantHide`
- **Built-in Validation:** Native constraint validation, `required`, `validationMessage`, and `expose` (`checkValidity`, `getValidationMessage`)
- **Rich Anatomy:** Supports `label`, `description`, `helperMessage`, `hasHtmlCode`, and `#default` / `#description` slots
- **Interactive & Loading States:** `disabled`, `readonly` (prevents native DOM toggling), `focus`, `loading`, and `isSkeleton`

**Common use cases:**

- Agreement with terms and privacy policies in forms
- Multi-option preferences and feature toggles
- Grouped single-choice options synchronized via `v-model:selected`
- Parent-child selection trees with `indeterminate` state
