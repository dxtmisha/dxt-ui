## ARIA Role and Live Region

`SnackbarItem` always sets `aria-atomic="true"` and automatically derives `role` and `aria-live` from status flags unless explicitly overridden:

- **`error`:** Defaults to `role="alert"` and `aria-live="assertive"` for critical errors requiring immediate announcement.
- **`success`:** Defaults to `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` for non-interrupting confirmations.
- **Explicit override (`role`, `ariaLive`):** Always takes precedence over `error` and `success` defaults (for example, `role="log"` or `aria-live="off"`).

<Source
    code={`
<template>
  <!-- Success notification (role="status", aria-live="polite") -->
  <SnackbarItem label="Download complete" success />

  <!-- Critical error (role="alert", aria-live="assertive") -->
  <SnackbarItem label="Connection error!" error />

  <!-- Explicit ARIA override -->
  <SnackbarItem label="Background event" role="log" aria-live="off" />
</template>
  `}
    language="html"
/>
