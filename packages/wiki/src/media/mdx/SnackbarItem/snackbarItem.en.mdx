The `SnackbarItem` component represents an individual notification card inside a `Snackbar` queue or as a standalone feedback banner. It manages the visual layout and accessibility semantics of a notification, including label, description, icons, body content, action buttons, and a close button.

**Key features:**

- **Structured Content:** Supports `label`, `description`, custom `html`, Vue `component` with `componentProps`, and `#default`, `#description`, and `#body` slots
- **Leading & Trailing Icons:** Full icon configuration including `icon`, `iconTrailing`, `iconTurn`, `iconHide`, `iconDir`, and `iconAttrs`
- **Action Controls:** Built-in single action `button` (`click`/`clickLite`) and multi-action bar via `actionsList`, `actionsSecondary`, `actionsHide`, and `actionsAttrs`
- **Dismissible Close Button:** Configurable `closeButton`, `iconClose`, `textClose`, and `value` emitted with the `close` event
- **WAI-ARIA Live Region:** Automatically applies `aria-atomic="true"`, defaults `error` to `role="alert"` / `aria-live="assertive"` and `success` to `role="status"` / `aria-live="polite"`, and supports explicit `role` and `ariaLive` overrides

**Common use cases:**

- Displaying short confirmation or status messages
- Notifications with "Undo" or "Retry" action buttons
- Error and success feedback cards with accessible live region announcements

> SnackbarItem is designed both as the default notification unit rendered by `Snackbar` and as a self-contained accessible notification card.
