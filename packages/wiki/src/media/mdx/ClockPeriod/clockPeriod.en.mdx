Component for selecting and toggling between AM (ante meridiem) and PM (post meridiem) time periods in a 12-hour clock format.

`ClockPeriod` provides a compact two-button selector for toggling between AM and PM. It can operate in standalone mode, bind two-way state via `v-model`, or automatically deduce the active period from a 24-hour `hour` value. It supports vertical and horizontal orientations, full keyboard navigation with arrow and toggle keys, custom period labels, and disabled or read-only states.

**Key features:**

- **Binary period selection**: Toggle between `'am'` and `'pm'` periods.
- **Two-way binding (`v-model`)**: Synchronize active period value with external state.
- **Automatic period resolution (`hour`)**: Pass hour numbers (`0..23`) to determine the period (`0..11` = AM, `12..23` = PM).
- **Layout orientations (`orientation`)**: Display buttons in `'vertical'` (default) or `'horizontal'` layout.
- **Keyboard navigation**: Support for `ArrowUp`/`ArrowLeft` (AM), `ArrowDown`/`ArrowRight` (PM), and `Space`/`Enter` to toggle.
- **Custom text labels**: Customizable period titles via `textAm` and `textPm` props.
- **Accessibility**: Built with `role="radiogroup"` and `role="radio"` semantics with `aria-checked` states.
- **Interactive states**: Support for `disabled` and `readonly` modes.

**Common use cases:**

- 12-hour time pickers alongside `ClockDial`.
- Scheduling, alarm settings, and appointment booking forms.
- Internationalized time inputs requiring 12-hour AM/PM distinction.
