## Thresholds & Accessibility (ARIA)

To prevent screen reader noise on every keystroke, `FieldCounter` activates its visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` region only when the remaining characters fall within `maxlengthOnce` (defaulting to 10% of `maxlength`) or when the limit is reached/exceeded.

**Properties:**

- `id` — explicit HTML ID for connecting via `aria-describedby` from form controls
- `maxlengthOnce` — remaining-character warning threshold (defaults to 10% of `maxlength`)
- `textCharacterRemaining` — announcement message with `[left]` placeholder (`Remaining [left] characters`)
- `textCharacterLimit` — announcement message when remaining characters ≤ 0 (`Character limit exceeded`)
