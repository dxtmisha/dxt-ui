The `Radio` component is an interactive form control for single-option selection from a mutually exclusive group or standalone value-variant toggling, with integrated validation, descriptions, and accessibility support.

It wraps a native `<input type="radio">` inside a semantic `<label>`, coordinating two-way `v-model:selected` and `v-model` synchronization, read-only click protection (`aria-readonly`), custom checked/indeterminate icons, loading indicators, and skeleton placeholders.

**Key features:**

- **Exclusive Group Selection:** Two-way `v-model:selected` synchronization with `valueVariant` and `valueVariantHide`, plus standalone `v-model` support
- **Built-in Validation:** Native constraint validation, `required`, `validationMessage`, and `expose` (`getValue`, `checkValidity`, `getValidationMessage`)
- **Rich Anatomy:** Supports `label`, `description`, `helperMessage`, `hasHtmlCode`, and `#default` / `#description` slots
- **Interactive & Loading States:** `disabled`, `readonly` (prevents native DOM toggling), `focus`, `loading`, and `isSkeleton`
- **Adaptive Layout:** Supports `block`, `itemCenter`, and `adaptive` alignment options

**Common use cases:**

- Selecting a shipping, payment, or subscription plan option
- Switching between mutually exclusive application modes
- Single-choice survey questions and preference forms
