- `back(): void` — Transition to the previous slide (`direction = 'back'`).
- `next(): void` — Transition to the next slide (`direction = 'next'`).
- `to(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide with automatic direction detection based on slide order.
- `top(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `Y` axis with `back` direction.
- `right(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `X` axis with `next` direction.
- `bottom(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `Y` axis with `next` direction.
- `left(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `X` axis with `back` direction.
- `down(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `Z` axis with `back` direction.
- `up(selected?: string): void` — Transition to the specified slide along the `Z` axis with `next` direction.
