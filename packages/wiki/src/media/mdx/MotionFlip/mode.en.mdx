## Usage Modes: Auto vs. Manual

MotionFlip provides two different modes for tracking layout changes: **Auto Mode** and **Manual Mode**. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right balance between simplicity and precise animation control.

### Auto Mode (Default)

By default, `auto` is `true`, so MotionFlip operates in automatic mode. In this mode, MotionFlip utilizes a `MutationObserver` (`childList: true`) to automatically detect when direct child elements are added, removed, or rearranged in the DOM.

**How it works:**
1. MotionFlip snapshots the initial positions and sizes of its child elements and monitors the container for `childList` mutations.
2. When you modify your list state (e.g., `items.value.push(newItem)`), Vue updates the DOM.
3. The `MutationObserver` detects the DOM change and automatically triggers the FLIP animation.

**Pros:**
- **Simpler Code:** You do not need to call the `update()` method or manage template refs. Mutate your reactive state and the animation runs automatically.
- **External Changes:** Works even when DOM changes are performed outside Vue reactivity.

**Cons:**
- **Continuous Observation:** Keeps a `MutationObserver` active while mounted and enabled.
- **Asynchronous Limitations:** Because `MutationObserver` fires *after* the DOM has already mutated, Auto Mode relies on the previously cached item measurements rather than capturing a fresh snapshot immediately before an update.

**When to use:** Ideal for standard dynamic lists, quick integrations, or when DOM mutations happen externally.

### Manual Mode

Manual mode is enabled by setting `:auto="false"`. In this mode, MotionFlip disables the `MutationObserver` and animates changes only when you explicitly call the exposed `update(callback)` method.

**How it works:**
1. You call `await motionFlipElement.value?.update(callback)`.
2. MotionFlip records the exact positions and sizes of all child elements *before* your callback runs.
3. Your callback executes, updating state and waiting for the DOM update (e.g., `await nextTick()`).
4. MotionFlip measures the *new* positions and smoothly animates elements from their old positions to the new ones.

**Pros:**
- **Maximum Performance:** Avoids continuous `MutationObserver` tracking.
- **Precision and Control:** Captures fresh initial coordinates right before each state change.
- **Graceful Interruptions:** Calling `update()` cancels any active transition cleanly and starts from the current visual state.

**When to use:** Recommended for complex lists, drag-and-drop interfaces, large grids, and animations triggered by programmatic actions.
