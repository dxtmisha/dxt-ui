Component for selecting and displaying time values on an interactive circular clock dial.

`ClockDial` provides a circular interface for choosing hours, minutes, and seconds. It supports standard 12-hour and 24-hour dual-ring hour selection, 60-division minute and second dials, continuous pointer tracking, an analog clock display mode (`clock`), hand rotation calculations, coordinate-based touch/click selection, stepping constraints (`step`), and customizable mark slots.

**Key features:**

- **Multiple dial modes (`type`)**: `12` (12-hour dial), `24` (24-hour dual-tier dial), `minute` (60-minute dial), and `second` (60-second dial).
- **Analog clock mode (`clock`)**: Displays hour (`hour`), minute (`minute`), and second (`second`) hands while disabling interactive selection.
- **Value and step constraints**: Minimum (`min`), maximum (`max`), and step (`step`) intervals.
- **Interactive selection**: Real-time dragging, touch gestures, and direct coordinate resolution (`selectByCoordinates`).
- **States**: Disabled (`disabled`) and read-only (`readonly`) modes.
- **Custom slots**: Custom rendering for division tick items (`item`) and central dial overlay (`default`).

**Common use cases:**

- Time pickers and alarm schedulers.
- Analog clock visualizations.
- Timer and countdown dials.
- Appointment and business hour selection.
