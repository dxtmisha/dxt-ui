## Analog clock hands mode (`clock`)

When the `clock` boolean property is enabled, the component switches to clock display mode without interactive selection. In this mode, individual time hands are rendered:

- `clock: boolean` — activates analog clock display mode with hands.
- `hour: number` — hour hand position (`arrowHour`) with continuous advancement based on minutes and seconds.
- `minute: number` — minute hand position (`arrowMinute`) with advancement based on seconds.
- `second: number` — second hand position (`arrowSecond`).

A center axis pin point (`point`) connects the hands at the center of the dial face.

<Source
    code={`
<template>
  <!-- Display analog time 10:15:30 -->
  <ClockDial
    clock
    type="12"
    :hour="10"
    :minute="15"
    :second="30"
  />
</template>
  `}
