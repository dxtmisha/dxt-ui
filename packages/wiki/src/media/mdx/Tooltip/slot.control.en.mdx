- `control(props: TooltipControl): any` — Scoped slot for the trigger element that controls the tooltip.

> **TooltipControl:**
> - `open: Ref<boolean>` — reactive open state.
> - `binds: TooltipControlBinds` — attributes and event listeners (`class`, `onClick`, `onMouseover`, `onMouseout`, and WAI-ARIA attributes `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` or `aria-describedby`).
