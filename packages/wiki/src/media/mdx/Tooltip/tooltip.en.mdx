The `Tooltip` component displays contextual pop-up hints relative to a trigger element on hover, click, or programmatic state changes.

It automatically calculates viewport-safe coordinates (`top` / `bottom` flipping and horizontal edge shifting), supports configurable show/hide delays, optional arrow pointers, interactive hover retention, inline `embedded` rendering, and complete WAI-ARIA bindings.

**Key features:**

- **Automatic Positioning:** Flips between bottom and top placement and shifts horizontally to stay inside the viewport
- **Optional Arrow Indicator:** Integrates with the `Arrow` constructor via `arrowShow`, `arrowPosition`, and `arrowAttrs`
- **Configurable Delays:** Fine-grained show delay (`delay`) and automatic hide timeout (`delayHide`)
- **Controlled & Programmatic Modes:** Control visibility via `:open`, `@tooltip`, or exposed `toggle(open, flash)`
- **Interactive & Embedded Modes:** Keep tooltip open on hover (`interactive`) or render inline without `Teleport` (`embedded` + `inDom`)
- **Rich Content & Slots:** Supports `label`, `description` (including numeric `0`), `#control`, `#default`, `#description`, and `#body` slots

**Common use cases:**

- Contextual explanations for icon-only buttons and controls
- Displaying full text for truncated headings or labels
- Brief helper tips paired with form controls or anchor copy actions
- Non-blocking interactive hint cards with links or structured body content
