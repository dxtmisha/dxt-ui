### `tooltip`

Triggered when the tooltip visibility state finishes opening or closing.

**Parameters:**

- `open: boolean` — visibility state (`true` when opened, `false` when closed)

<Source
    code={`
<template>
  <Tooltip label="Status hint" @tooltip="(open) => console.log('tooltip:', open)">
    <template #control="{ binds }">
      <button v-bind="binds" type="button">Hover me</button>
    </template>
  </Tooltip>
</template>
  `}
    language="html"
/>
