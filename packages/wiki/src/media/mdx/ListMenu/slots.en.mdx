### `head`

Slot for rendering the menu trigger element. Receives `WindowControlItem` properties (`class`, `open`, `binds`, `classesWindow`, `onClick`, `onKeydown`, `onContextmenu`). Bind `binds` directly to your trigger element to enable automatic click, keyboard, and ARIA support.

### `list`

Slot for rendering the menu body content displayed inside the `Window` when the submenu is open.
